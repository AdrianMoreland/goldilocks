## Purpose
Order stock from a supplier at a price that still gives the customer an agreed quote.
Sources: Merrion Gold Procedures (point 11), Baird standing settlement instructions.

## Suppliers
- Named in the procedures: Amark and Baird.
- Baird & Co (London) holds gold, silver, platinum and palladium accounts. Settlement details and contacts are in the Baird settlement instructions file. Do not copy bank details into any SOP.
- Orders and hedges are placed on StoneX or CoinInvest by a manager ([[payment-lock-and-hedge#steps]]).

## Steps
1. Get the price from the supplier.
2. While the supplier is still on the phone, work out the customer price with our margin.
3. Agree that price with the customer.
4. Ring the supplier back to lock the price for the product and for delivery to our location.
5. Record the purchase order in Business Central ([[bc-purchase-orders]]).
6. When the goods arrive, follow [[desk-security-and-dual-control#deliveries-in]].
