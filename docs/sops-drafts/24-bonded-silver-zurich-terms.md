## Purpose
The fee formula and terms for silver stored in the bonded warehouse of Loomis International in Zurich. Adds detail to [[bonded-silver-storage]]. Several figures differ from that SOP and are marked.
Sources: Agreement for Bonded Storage at Loomis Zurich, Bonded Storage Info, Loomis release order form, sample bonded invoice.

## Storage fee
1. The annual fee is 1% plus VAT, charged quarterly in arrears.
2. The quarterly fee is 0.25% plus VAT of the value of the silver stored, or €45 plus VAT, whichever is greater.
3. Value is the average value of the silver throughout the quarter, using the LBMA Silver Price fix set daily at 12:00 BST each working day. This differs from [[bonded-silver-storage#storage-fee]], which uses the value at the time of billing.
4. Fees are calculated and paid in euro unless agreed before purchase.
5. Silver bought part-way through a quarter is charged for the days stored.

## Withdrawal and VAT
1. Silver can be withdrawn by the customer or by Merrion Gold on the customer's behalf. Merrion Gold can bring it to the Dublin premises.
2. VAT is payable on silver leaving a Loomis location. It is not payable if the silver is sold back to Merrion Gold without being withdrawn.
3. A one-off delivery, handling and administration fee of €100 plus VAT applies to each withdrawal. Higher-value shipments may cost more, agreed before shipment. [TODO: the €100 fee is in the Loomis agreement only. Confirm.]

## Selling back
1. The customer can sell back by phone or email and does not need to visit the warehouse.
2. Merrion Gold buys back any silver bought through it. Proceeds go to the nominated account within three business days of the sale.
3. Sale is not guaranteed outside 08:45 to 17:00 Monday to Friday or on public or bank holidays.
4. VAT paid at purchase is not refundable when selling back.

## Documents
1. Merrion Gold issues an invoice for the purchase. Serial numbers can be requested and appear on the invoice.
2. The invoice should state the bar serial number, the total ounces, and that a storage fee of 1% per annum plus VAT applies.
3. Bonded silver confirmations are in [[confirmation-letters#bonded-silver-confirmation-of-holdings]].

## Visits and services
- Warehouse visits are arranged through Merrion Gold with at least five working days' notice.
- Account audits and weighing of metal are priced on request.
