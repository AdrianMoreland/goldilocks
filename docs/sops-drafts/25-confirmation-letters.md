## Purpose
Standard letters customers ask for as proof of funds, holdings, value or transactions. The company's past letters were reduced to one wording skeleton per type. Use [placeholders]; never reuse another customer's details.

## General rules
1. Use the Merrion Gold letterhead. Greet "Dear [Mr or Ms Surname]", or "To Whom It May Concern" when the letter is for a third party.
2. State the date and the exact amounts. Close with "Please let me know if you require any further information."
3. Do not put customer personal data, bank details or account numbers in anything other than the letter itself.

## Funds held on account
Use when a customer needs evidence of money held pending a purchase.
1. Wording: "This is confirmation of your funds which are held on account with Merrion Gold. As at [date], you hold a total of €[amount] on account with Merrion Gold."
2. Add: the funds can be transferred, in full or part, to the customer's bank account on request, or used for future orders.
3. Signed by the broker. See [[payment-lock-and-hedge#funds-left-on-account]].

## Cheque received
Use when a cheque arrives, before it clears.
1. Wording: "We have received a cheque for €[amount] made payable to Merrion Gold Ltd on [date]. No purchases may be made and no prices locked in until the cheque has cleared."
2. Add: once cleared, the balance is available at a time of the customer's choosing, there is no time limit, no obligation to purchase, and the money can be returned.
3. Signed by the broker.

## Confirmation of card lodgement
- "[Customer] has lodged €[amount] on account with Merrion Gold via card payment and now holds the money on account." Signed by the Head of Trading. Card payments carry a 2% handling fee ([[payment-lock-and-hedge]]).

## Confirmation of payment and amount invested
- "Merrion Gold received a payment of €[amount] from you on [date]. These funds were used to purchase [qty] x [size] gold bar, 999.9 purity, 24 carat, at €[price] each, totalling €[total]." Signed by the broker.

## Confirmation of lodgement of metal
Use when a customer leaves coins or bars without selling.
1. State the date and what was lodged.
2. State: this is not a sale and no price has been agreed or fixed. There is no time limit. The items can be withdrawn without penalty during opening hours, or sold to Merrion Gold when the customer decides.
3. Include signature blocks for the customer and for Merrion Gold.

## Confirmation of stock in the vault
Use when a customer who stores sealed boxes withdraws, swaps or returns items. State the date, what moved, the new holding, and how many boxes are sealed and how many opened.

## Bonded silver confirmation of holdings
1. "Merrion Gold confirms that [customer] holds the following silver bars in storage with Loomis International in their bonded warehouse in Zurich: [quantity and weight, refiner, serial numbers]."
2. Add the balance sheet paragraph below.
3. Add: a storage fee of 1% plus VAT per annum applies, billed quarterly.

## Balance sheet confirmation
Wording: bars stored on the customer's behalf at Loomis International in Zurich do not lie on the balance sheet of Merrion Gold Limited or of Loomis International. They are fully allocated in the customer's name in the Merrion Gold section of the warehouse and lie outside the banking system. If either company went into liquidation or administration, the receiver or administrator could not use or hold the metal. Signed by the Head of Trading. ## Valuation letters
1. Say which price source is used: LBMA gold AM fix, PM fix or silver fix, with the date.
2. Types: position summary; live value of bars (serial number, refinery, ounces, value); total holdings (gold, silver, total); single item.
3. Check that each price line is labelled gold or silver correctly. A past letter labelled a gold fix as silver.
4. Value bars at the bid price from the product table ([[pricing#buy-price]]), not at a fixed percentage of spot.

## Net asset value (NAV)
1. NAV is the value of the customer's holdings less outstanding liabilities, meaning quarterly storage fees due.
2. The customer asks by email. Use the most recent fix unless they specify AM or PM. Silver uses the LBMA silver fix.

## Transaction statement
- "This is a statement of your transactions with Merrion Gold from [month year] to [month year]." Table of date, product, total. Signed by the broker. Used for yearly or tax records.

## Transaction receipt
After a customer sells to us: "Transaction Receipt, Reference P[YYYYMMDD]-[NN]. On [date], [customer] sold the following to Merrion Gold for a total of €[total]: [qty] x [item] at €[each]." Signed by the broker with email and phone.

## Authority letter
"[Name] has the authority to act on behalf of Merrion Gold Limited in all matters regarding the purchase and sale of gold bullion." Signed by the Managing Director. Issued when a supplier or refiner requires proof of who may trade.

## Ownership confirmation
- Confirms who owns Merrion Gold when a bank, supplier or customer asks. The past letter listed the owners' dates of birth and home addresses. Do not include these without management approval.
