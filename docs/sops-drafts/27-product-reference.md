## Purpose
Answer customer questions about bars and coins. Specifications are from the Irish staff FAQ and the coin descriptions. Weights are in troy ounces unless stated. One troy ounce is 31.1035 grams ([[pricing#sell-price]]).

## Gold bars
1. Sizes: 1oz (31.1g), 50g, 100g, 250g, 10oz (311g), 500g, 1kg. A 12.5kg bar is available on request.
2. Mostly Valcambi, PAMP, Argor-Heraeus and C. Hafner.
3. All bars are 24 carat, 99.99%.
4. Bars come with an authenticity certificate and a unique serial number. Coins have neither, but all gold is tested.
5. Bars carry a lower premium than coins. Larger products and larger orders have a lower premium per unit.

## Refineries on the approved list
Royal Canadian Mint, PAMP, Credit Suisse, Argor Heraeus, Valcambi, Perth Mint, Umicore, C. Hafner.

## Tax points
- Investment gold is VAT-exempt ([[pricing#vat]]).
- Royal Mint coins such as the Sovereign and the gold and silver Britannia are exempt from capital gains tax in the UK. The customer is responsible for their own tax ([[customer-terms-summary]]).

## Testing
Every item is weighed and tested before it is given to a customer ([[buyback-paperwork-and-testing#testing]]).

## Cast and minted bars
Cast bars are poured from molten metal and look uneven. Minted bars are cut from blanks and are smooth. Minted bars usually cost more.

## Points not to use until checked
The training cheat sheet has statements that look wrong and must be verified before use:
- Metalor listed as a Japanese refiner (Metalor is Swiss).
- Argor-Heraeus described as German in the FAQ (it is a Swiss refiner).
- "A troy ounce is 3.1g heavier than a regular ounce" (about 2.75g).
- Britannia carat history overlaps (22 carat 1987 to 2013 and 24 carat from 2013).
