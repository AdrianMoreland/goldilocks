## Purpose
Handle weekend orders when markets are closed. Source: MG Weekend Prices Sheet, a past sheet used as an example.

## Steps
1. A fixed price sheet is issued for the weekend. Quote only from the sheet.
2. Take the customer's name and contact details.
3. Tell the customer all items are available for collection from Monday.
4. Merrion Gold contacts the customer on Monday.
5. The price is locked only when funds land ([[payment-lock-and-hedge]]).
