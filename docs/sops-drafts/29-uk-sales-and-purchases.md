## Purpose
How the UK, Scottish and Northern Ireland branches (Shavo Services Ltd: Scottish Bullion, British Bullion, Belfast Bullion) quote, sell and buy. The UK still uses Zoho and the trade sheet, not Business Central.
Source: Shavo Services Bullion Manual 2026.

## Pricing
1. Update the spot price before every live estimate.
2. Use the UK pricing sheet. Give discounts only if applicable.
3. For a cash purchase add 2% to the rate in Zoho.
4. Silver, platinum and palladium carry 20% VAT. Investment gold is VAT-free.
5. Silver bought from a supplier or from Belfast: show VAT separately from the rate in Zoho.
6. Silver bought from a customer under the Margin Scheme: add the VAT onto the rate and do not show VAT separately.
7. Do not sell Belfast stock to mainland UK customers without checking with Head Office.
8. Inter-branch parcel charge in the Zoho rate: under £500, add £20. Over £500 up to £30,000, add £55. [TODO: confirm the band wording and the charge above £30,000.]
9. Ounces = grams ÷ 31.1.

## Information to take on every call, email or chat
1. Full name and phone or email.
2. Buying or selling, and the items.
3. Payment method. Remind the customer there is a 2% cash fee. We do not accept cards.
4. For sales to us, the payment timeframe.
5. Immediate collection or order-in (delivery timeframe from Head Office).
6. Branch, date and time, and your initials on the appointment.
7. Only hold stock if the customer is coming in that day.

## Payment methods (customer buying)
1. Bank transfer: details are at the bottom of every quote. It can take up to two hours to arrive. Send the invoice after confirmation.
2. Currencies: GBP by default. For EUR or USD, contact Head Office so the Zoho quote is amended.
3. Cash: tell the customer about the 2% fee when booking. EUR or USD cash has a 7% currency conversion charge.
4. Cheque or banker's draft: payable to Shavo Services Ltd, sent by the end of the next working day to the Belfast office. Allow 4 working days to clear before dispatch.
5. Cash needs the checks in [[uk-aml-and-cash]].

## Customer buying from us
1. New customer form. Zoho Books → Sales → Customers → New customer. Record name, email, phone, age, gender, customer type, average spend, language. Currency GBP unless told otherwise.
2. Zoho Sales → Quotes → New Estimate. Select customer and branch.
3. Create a trade sheet line with the branch reference (for example GV-G20261002 for gold). Put "Quote only" in the CPTY column until the sale is final.
4. Print the quote or email it with Save and Send. Sign off with name, "Bullion Broker" and branch.
5. When the customer pays, convert the estimate to an invoice (draft), Mark as Sent, Record a Payment with the correct method, then save and send. If the price changed, edit the estimate before converting.
6. Complete the trade sheet. In the UK Gold tab: copy the counterparty reference and cost from the stock sheet when selling from stock; the Zoho total goes in Receipt; Locked in = YES; AML = YES if cash (due-diligence form) or N/A if bank transfer; Paid = CASH, EFT or CHEQUE. Silver goes in the UK - Silver tab. Margin Scheme silver goes in the UK - MS tab.
7. Tell the bullion team when the trade is done, for hedging.
8. After collection, highlight the customer name and the collection columns green.
9. Update the UK Bullion Stock Availability sheet and the stock sheet.
10. If the customer opted in, add them to Mailchimp. Send the review email from the branch template.

## Customer selling to us
1. Take a copy of ID and the other documents. Wear white gloves. Check markings and condition. For bars the serial number must match the packaging and certificate.
2. Create the vendor: Zoho Inventory → Purchase → Vendor.
3. Give an initial quote first. Confirm pricing and the payment timeframe with Head Office.
4. If the customer proceeds, create a Purchase Order in Zoho. Trade sheet references are UK-GP (gold) or UK-SP (silver).
5. Small amounts: test immediately with the customer seated. Large quantities or if unsure: give the customer a copy of the PO and say we will be in touch.
6. After the item passes testing, lock in the price. The customer emails their bank details to Accounts for every sale, even repeat sales. Payment is by bank transfer only, to a UK bank account in the name of the customer attending.
7. Give the customer a copy of the PO (mark Issued in Zoho). Keep the signed copy.
8. Complete the trade sheet: quantity, product, spot, date received and tested, total price paid, price per item, total ounces (grams ÷ 31.1), date paid. Notify Head Office for hedging.
9. Photograph the items. Attach ID, proof of address and proof of ownership to the PO. Update stock sheets.
10. Seal the items in a tamper-proof bag marked with date, reference and item name, with two signatures.

## Filing
- Buyers: New Customer Form, Zoho invoice, valid photo ID, collection receipt if collection is on another date, and for £8,000 or more cumulative also proof of address and proof of funds (each under 3 months old), and the due-diligence form with cash bands.
- Sellers: New Customer Form, Zoho PO, photo ID, and for £8,000 or more cumulative proof of address and proof of ownership or funds.
