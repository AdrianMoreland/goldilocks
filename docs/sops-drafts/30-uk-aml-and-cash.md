## Purpose
AML checks, cash counting and safe rules for the UK branches (Shavo Services Ltd). Shavo is a High Value Dealer under HMRC and follows the Money Laundering Regulations 2017, the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 and the Terrorism Act 2000.
Sources: Shavo Services Bullion Manual 2026, Shavo Services AML Risk Assessment (Nov 2023).

## Thresholds
1. A Due Diligence Transaction Form is completed for all cash transactions. A copy goes with the transaction record.
2. Cash transactions over £8,000 are filed separately in the "8,000+" folder.
3. For £8,000 or more cumulative, take proof of address and proof of funds or ownership, each less than 3 months old.
4. The £8,000 limit was lowered from £10,000 in the Nov 2023 risk assessment. The older Scottish Bullion policy still says £10,000 and is out of date.
5. Do not accept cash from third parties or from customers who do not visit a branch.

## Accepted ID
- Current passport, UK or international.
- Full UK driving licence. The name must match the invoice.
- Armed Forces ID.
- Non-UK citizen identity or residency card.
- Use the ID scanner at the branch for all bullion sales. If it flags the ID, the transaction cannot go ahead.

## Accepted proof of address (less than 3 months old)
- Utility bill or bank statement.
- Annual council tax bill, only for the current residence.
- HMRC tax return.
- A doctor's or solicitor's letter, only if no other proof is available.

## Accepted proof of funds (buying) and ownership (selling)
- Funds: recent bank or savings statement, withdrawal receipt, will or copy confirming cash inheritance, solicitor letter confirming inheritance, proof of cash sale of property, vehicle or assets.
- Ownership: invoice or PO, receipt, inheritance documents, bank statement showing the original purchase, solicitor letter.

## Companies
Record full name, Companies House number, registered office, principal business address, the directors (for private or unlisted companies) and beneficial owners above 25%.

## Suspicious transactions
1. Complete the transaction normally while discreetly gathering information.
2. Tell Head Office straight away so a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) can be made to the National Crime Agency.
3. Do not alert the customer and do not put yourself in danger.
4. If cash paperwork is missing, tell Head Office so a SAR can be considered.
5. Records are kept for 5 years.

## Four-eye policy
1. All bullion and cash handling needs two people. A lone worker contacts Head Office to be a second person remotely.
2. Do not open gold packages or handle cash without a second person.
3. Count cash through the cash counter with two people. Finish all stages of one transaction before starting another task.

## Cash and safe handling
1. Verified cash of £5,000 or more: seal in a Pivotal bag, place in the drop safe, record the total and both signatures.
2. Under £5,000: store in the bullion safe in a sealed Pivotal bag until the threshold is reached.
3. Never leave cash on a desk or unsecured. Hand all cash over at every scheduled collection.
4. On the cash transaction sheet, highlight the lines going to banking and have both staff initial the sheet.
5. Pivotal bag label: from Shavo Services Ltd, the branch address, date collected, to Pivotal Cash Processing, the Pivotal cash centre, and the amount.
6. Pivotal collection: verify the driver's identity, dual-lock the cash safe, sign the receipt and keep a copy. Report any discrepancy to Head Office straight away.
7. Collection schedule: weekly for GV, LV, BF and ED; every two weeks for BR and NOTT; monthly for RD, OL and SH. [TODO: confirm what each branch code stands for.]
8. Bullion safe: stock in the lower safe, extra silver in the main section. The safe is opened only for bullion appointments, virtual stock checks and storing new purchases, Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm. Customers must not be near the vault during a Pivotal collection or safe access.
