## Purpose
Receive, send and hand over bullion so every step is photographed and evidenced for insurance and legal claims.
Source: Shavo Services Bullion Manual 2026.

## Photos and video
Photos and video are mandatory for every delivery and collection.

## Inbound courier
1. Sign for the parcel and place it under the nominated camera.
2. Open it immediately with two colleagues.
3. Photograph all sides of the outer packaging.
4. One colleague videos the opening. The video may be deleted after 5 days.
5. Log the contents with time, date, signature and countersignature.
6. Photograph the items. Update the UK Trade Sheet. Test for authenticity.

## Outbound parcel
1. Prepare the box. Label it if it goes by FedEx.
2. Include paperwork listing the contents and references.
3. The second colleague photographs the bullion and the label. The first packs while the second records under CCTV.
4. Seal the box and leave it under the camera until the courier arrives.
5. Record the timestamp. Delete the video 5 days after the destination confirms receipt.

## Customer collection
1. Prepare an itemised collection receipt, with bar serial numbers if they are not on the Zoho invoice.
2. Verify ID. Keep a copy if the purchase was by email or phone.
3. Show the items without handing them over.
4. The customer signs and dates two copies of the receipt. Keep one.
5. Hand the items over. Offer the mailing list and a review.
6. Update the trade sheet and send the review email from the branch mail account.
