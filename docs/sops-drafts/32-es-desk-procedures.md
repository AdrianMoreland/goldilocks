## Purpose
Summary of the Spanish desk procedures (Spanish-language Training ESP document), translated. The Spanish branches use Zoho and the monthly trade sheet.

## Estimates
1. Open the Monthly Tradesheet, tab "ES - Gold". One line per product: client, quantity, order, date, spot at the time of enquiry.
2. Enter "Quote Only" in CPTY and Locked In until the sale is final.
3. Enter ALL SOLD when every item on the P-Line is sold. If only part is sold, adjust the quantity.
4. In Zoho, check whether the customer exists. If not, create them with name, display name, email and phone.
5. New Transaction → Estimate. The reference is the next trade sheet line number.
6. Add products, quantity and price. For silver enter the price without VAT, then add VAT. [TODO: the source says 23%. The standard Spanish rate is believed to be 21%. Confirm.]
7. Check prices, then Save and Send by email.

## Selling to a customer
1. After the sale fill in the payment columns of the trade sheet.
2. CPTY: the P-Line reference from the purchases tab if the item is in stock. If it must be ordered, post a message in the Merrion Gold Teams chat.
3. Cost: the Price/Item from the purchases tab, or the supplier invoice if ordered.
4. Receipt: the amount paid excluding VAT.
5. Select Profit, Margin, Locked In, Total Price and AML. Enter the hedged figure in the sold column. Drag the formulas down. For silver also select VAT Due.
6. Cash over €10,000, or linked cash cumulatively over €10,000 in one year: take photo ID, proof of address and source of funds.
7. Tell the Merrion Gold team about any order or stock problem.

## Buying from a customer
1. Ask for valid photo ID and the items: type, quantity and metal.
2. Fill in the trade sheet: client, quantity, product, date received, spot price, price paid, price per item, ounces.
3. In Zoho Inventory, check whether the customer is a vendor. If not, Contacts → Vendors → New.
4. Create a Purchase Order. The reference is the P-Line from the trade sheet.
5. Take prices from the price book. Check metal prices daily.
6. Save as draft, print, give to the customer to complete and sign, and keep a copy.
7. Tell the customer payment will be made within up to 10 working days.
8. When the items are received and checked, change the PO from Draft to Received.
