## Summary
The Irish branches have moved from Zoho and Google Sheets to Microsoft Dynamics Business Central (BC). This SOP covers Ireland only. The UK and Spanish branches still use Zoho and Google Sheets (see [[systems-overview#other-branches]]).

## System map
| Task | System |
|---|---|
| Customer inquiries | Outlook, website form, WhatsApp, phone, walk-in |
| Live spot prices and product prices | Goldilocks pricing dashboard; Google Sheets pricing workbook (legacy) |
| Quotes, invoices, customer purchases, supplier shipments | Business Central |
| Customer ID photo and collection signature | Business Central, captured on the signature tablet |
| Orders created before BC go-live | Zoho Books (read-only) |
| Stock counts | Business Central ([[stock-management]]) |
| Hedging and supplier orders | Placed by managers |
| Internal communication | Teams, Outlook |
| Calendar | Google Calendar |
| HR, leave, rota | BrightHR |
| Website | WordPress |
| Shared documents | Google Drive |

## BC and Zoho
BC went live in Ireland in September 2026 and the desk is still in transition. Orders were not migrated, so each order exists in only one system.
1. Create all new quotes and invoices in BC. Never create new documents in Zoho.
2. To find an existing order, search BC first. If it isn't there, it was created before go-live: search Zoho.
3. If the order can't be found in either system, escalate to the branch manager.

## Customer ID and signatures
- The customer's ID photo and collection signature are captured on the signature tablet, triggered from BC. They are stored against the order in BC.
- Never photograph customer ID on a personal phone or save it to Google Drive, email or Teams.

## Data access
- Google Drive is broadly accessible to employees. Do not store customer ID, payment details or other customer personal data there.

## Other branches
The UK and Spanish branches use Zoho and Google Sheets for quotes, invoices and stock. The IE SOPs in this Knowledge Center do not apply to them until they move to BC.
