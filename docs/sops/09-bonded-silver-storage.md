## Purpose
Offer customers VAT-free silver held in a bonded warehouse.

## What we offer
- Silver held in bond is VAT-free while it stays in the bonded warehouse.
- Storage costs 1% per year of the value of the customer's holdings.

## Storage fee
- The fee is calculated and billed quarterly: 0.25% of the holdings' value at the time of billing, not the value at purchase.
- Tell the customer that the fee rises and falls with the silver price.

## Serial numbers
1. Every bonded bar has a serial number.
2. When a customer buys bonded silver, allocate specific serials to them on the bonded serials spreadsheet.
3. Update the customer's invoice to show the allocated serials and send it to the customer.
4. Serial tracking will move to BC once it is set up. Until then, the spreadsheet is the record.

## Taking silver out of bond
1. The customer asks to take delivery of their silver.
2. Calculate 23% VAT on the value of the silver at the time of removal.
3. The customer pays the VAT. Funds must land before release ([[payment-lock-and-hedge#confirming-funds]]).
4. Arrange release from the warehouse and update the serials spreadsheet.

## Selling bonded silver back
- Treat it as a normal buyback at the bid price ([[customer-buyback]]). No VAT is due.
- The silver stays in bond. Reallocate the serials on the spreadsheet.
