## Purpose
Answer questions like "Is gold going up?" honestly and consistently, without making predictions.

## Rules
1. Never predict the price or give a price target.
2. Never say or imply that gold "will" go up.
3. You may give recommendations and explanations, e.g. which product suits the customer's goal. Always make clear that we are not financial advisers.
4. Always mention the buy/sell spread to customers who may sell within a year.

## What to look at
- Trend: is spot above or below its 50-day and 200-day moving averages?
- Real interest rates (US 10-year TIPS yield). Falling real yields usually support gold.
- US dollar strength. For euro customers, also EUR/USD.
- Interest rate expectations.
- Central bank buying (World Gold Council data).
- Gold ETF flows.
- Geopolitical events. These often cause short spikes that fade.

## What to say
"We're not financial advisers, and no one can reliably call short-term moves. What I can tell you is the current backdrop: [factors]. Most of our customers treat gold as a long-term hold and a hedge rather than a trade, and buying in stages smooths out timing risk."

## Then ask
- How long do you plan to hold it?
- Bars or coins?
