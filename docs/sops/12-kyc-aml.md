## Purpose
Know who the customer is before we deal with them, and keep a record that proves it. Merrion Gold is a High Value Goods Dealer (HVGD) supervised by the Department of Justice for anti-money-laundering (AML).
Sources: AML Policy (Dec 2022), MG AML Risk Policy (Nov 2023), KYC-AML Checklist.

## When checks are required
1. Every cash transaction, or series of cash transactions by the same customer, of €10,000 or more: full checks in [[cash-transactions-ie]] before you accept the cash.
2. Every purchase from a customer (buyback): photo ID only. No KYC pack is needed unless cash of €10,000 or more is involved ([[buyback-paperwork-and-testing]]).
3. Every collection: photo ID at the desk ([[customer-collection#identity-check]]).
4. Whenever anything looks unusual, at any amount ([[suspicious-transaction-reporting]]).
5. A full KYC pack (the steps below) is needed only for cash of €10,000 or more. Other customers need photo ID at collection, and nothing more unless something looks unusual.

## Customer due diligence (CDD) steps
1. Do the checks before the business relationship starts or the cash is accepted.
2. Identify the customer from valid photo ID and verify it (see [[kyc-aml#accepted-photo-id]]).
3. Confirm their address (see [[kyc-aml#accepted-proof-of-address]]).
4. Ask whether the buyer is the ultimate owner of the metal. If not, identify the beneficial owner the same way (see [[kyc-aml#companies-and-beneficial-owners]]).
5. Ask the purpose of the purchase and where the money comes from. For cash of €10,000 or more, evidence is required ([[cash-transactions-ie#source-of-funds]]).
6. Take clean, legible copies of the documents. The copy must show the customer's name (and the beneficial owner's name, if any).
7. Complete the KYC-AML Checklist (see [[kyc-aml#kyc-aml-checklist-fields]]) and save the document pack as one PDF in the AML folder.
8. The MLRO signs off the KYC pack for cash of €10,000 or more.

## Accepted photo ID
- Current passport, Irish or international.
- Driving licence (photocard), Irish or international.
- National identity card; EU member state ID card.
- Garda-signed photo ID.
- Social Welfare card with photo.
- National Age Card.

## Accepted proof of address
- Current Revenue or Department of Social Protection document showing name and PPS number.
- Utility bill, bank statement or mortgage statement showing the current address, dated within the last 3 months.
- Current local authority document.
- Electoral register confirmation.
- Revenue tax notice dated within the last 3 months.
- Court appointment document (liquidator, grant of probate).
- "Recent" means dated within the last 3 months.

## Companies and beneficial owners
- For a company, record: full name, CRO number, registered office address, principal business address.
- For a private (unlisted) company also record the list of directors and of beneficial owners.
- A beneficial owner is anyone who owns or controls more than 25% (25% plus one share) of the company, or controls it in another way. The same test applies to partnerships and trusts.
- Take photo ID from two directors, or from the sole director and the company secretary, plus ID for the person giving instructions.
- Company evidence: latest report and accounts, and details of changes of office or shareholding not shown on the public register.
- People being identified should come to the office with their documents so copies can be taken.

## Face-to-face only
- All orders are collected in person with photo ID. There are no online sales, no intermediaries, no third-party payers and no delivery to addresses.
- Any request that avoids face-to-face contact is a red flag ([[suspicious-transaction-reporting#red-flags]]).

## Customers known to Merrion Vaults
Merrion Vaults safe-deposit customers have already given photo ID and proof of address to the sister company. Cash checks are the same whether or not the customer is known to Merrion Vaults ([[cash-transactions-ie#when-it-applies]]).

## KYC-AML checklist fields
1. Client: name, date of birth, country of residence, nationality; photo ID attached; proof of address attached; PEP checks required (Y/N); additional checks attached (Y/N/NA).
2. Beneficial owner: full name; details attached.
3. Payment: method (EFT, cheque or cash); currency; for cash, denominations, proof of source attached, stated source of funds; counterparty bank.
4. Confirmations: all documents attached; PDF saved in the AML folder; staff name and signature.
5. The KYC Onboarding sheet adds: country of the originating bank account, further account checks, bank statements provided, ongoing due diligence required, and signatures of the Compliance Officer and the broker.
