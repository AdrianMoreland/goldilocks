## Purpose
Make sure no one at Merrion Gold gives, takes or asks for a bribe.
Source: Anti-Corruption and Anti-Bribery Policy (Nov 2023 update).

## Who it applies to
Directors and all employees, including contract and temporary staff. Read it together with the AML policy ([[aml-governance-and-training]]) and the Code of Conduct. The law is the Prevention of Corruption Acts 1889 to 2010.

## What counts as bribery
Offering, promising, giving, accepting or asking for any inducement (gift, loan, fee or reward) to gain an advantage. Facilitation payments are not allowed.

## If someone asks for a payment
1. Ask for proof that the payment is legitimate.
2. Ask for an official receipt. If there is none, refuse and say company policy forbids it.
3. Record what happened and tell your line manager.
4. If unsure, ask your line manager before doing anything.

## Gifts and entertainment
- Modest gifts and entertainment are fine and do not need written approval.
- Disproportionate or inappropriate gifts are not allowed.
- Cash and cash equivalents (vouchers, gift cards, shares) are always inappropriate.

## Raising a concern
- Reports are confidential and made in good faith without reprisal.
- Tell your line manager if unsure.
