## Purpose
Protect physical gold and cash by always working in pairs, under camera, with a record.
Source: Merrion Gold Procedures (18-point desk procedure).

## Dual control
- Two members of staff are required for deliveries, and for moving gold into or out of the Gold Safety Deposit Box.
- Cash of €10,000 or more is also counted with a second person ([[cash-transactions-ie#before-accepting-the-cash]]).

## Cameras
All deliveries, testing and dealings involving gold are done under the department cameras.

## Gold room
- Physical gold deals (buying from customers and selling to customers) are done in the Gold room.
- A partition wall with a service window and transfer area separates staff and customer.

## Deliveries in
1. Sign for every delivery and bring it to the office straight away.
2. Open packages at the Merrion Gold desk under the camera.
3. One staff member checks the contents and a second verifies them.
4. Check the serial number of each bar against the delivery docket.
5. Both staff initial the delivery docket.
6. Test the gold ([[buyback-paperwork-and-testing#testing]]).
7. Record the goods in Business Central ([[bc-purchase-orders]]).

## Storage in the gold safety deposit box
1. All gold is kept in a pre-allocated Safety Deposit Box.
2. The box keys are kept in the department safe.
3. Two staff are present whenever gold is stored in or removed from the box.
4. A customer order waiting for collection is held for at most 30 days ([[customer-collection]]).

## Security bags
1. Each customer order goes in its own security bag.
2. Write on the bag in capital letters: the transaction reference code, the customer name and the contents.
3. Keep bagged orders in the customer safe ([[stock-management#customer-safe-cst-safe]]).

## Transaction reference code
- Write the transaction reference code on the bag, for example DU-G202610823.

## Gold mailbox
- The shared Gold mailbox is checked constantly.
- Answer every gold email query as soon as possible ([[customer-email-wording]]).
