## Purpose
Answer customer questions about safe deposit boxes at Merrion Vaults and know where the two companies' responsibilities divide.
Sources: Merrion Vaults Conditions of Licence, Box Sizes list, vault information letter.

## Who is who
- Merrion Vaults is Shavo Ltd trading as Merrion Vaults. Merrion Gold Limited is a separate company at the same office. Both have the same two owners.
- Merrion Vaults holds the box. Merrion Gold does not hold the customer's box contents and has no knowledge of them.
- Metal bought from Merrion Gold can be handed over in the vault with both parties present.

- Insurance for contents up to €500,000 per box is available at an extra charge.
- Two pension funds need two separate boxes.

## Opening a box at collection
Setting up a box takes about fifteen minutes and can be done with colleagues at the time a customer collects an order. Box sizes can be viewed in person.

## Access and security
1. Vault access uses a biometric retina scan. Each person also gets a keycard for reception but cannot enter the vault without the scan.
2. Dual-access can be set so the box opens only with two or more named people present. For a company or trustee, recommend more than one nominated person.
3. A full access log (dates, times, individuals) can be requested.
4. A company licensee accesses only through registered users. Adding or removing a user needs a board resolution or signed appointment.
5. Access is during opening hours only, by a registered user with a key whose identity is verified.
6. Merrion Vaults keeps no key. Keys are its property. A lost key must be reported in writing and the customer pays for the replacement lock and keys.
7. Access is refused if the licence term has ended or fees are outstanding, or if the user is impaired or identity is doubtful.
8. Premises are open for access for not less than 25 hours in every seven days.
9. An executor or personal representative with probate, or a liquidator or receiver, can be given access on evidence.
10. Recommend customers tell their solicitor or name the box in their will.

## Terms customers should know
1. The licence renews automatically for the same period unless the customer ends it in writing on or before expiry, and not later than 7 days after.
2. No refunds for early termination.
3. Payment is due at the start of each period. Interest runs on sums unpaid after 30 days. Unpaid in 30 days, the licence can be ended.
4. Within 7 days after the end, return keys and remove contents. If keys are not returned, an administration fee of €55 applies. After 30 days Merrion Vaults may drill the box and dispose of contents to recover amounts due.
5. Forbidden contents: explosives, corrosive, dangerous or illegal substances, liquids, gases, firearms, perishables, and anything unlawful.
6. Liability is capped at the annual licence fee, unless the optional insurance applies. Insurance covers contents up to the stated limit.
7. Claims must be made in writing as soon as reasonably possible and substantiated within 30 days.
8. Irish law applies.
9. The deposit is set in the contract particulars. Ask a manager.
